Seraph is a digital artist.

He is a Palestinian, Filipino and transgender artist. He seeks to explore the themes of his own identity through a transhumanist lens.

He specializes in character design and concept art. Illustration too if he feels like it.

Transhumanism is an interest. What separates the flesh from machine? The body is a well designed machine. Less than divine but close to perfection.

Growing up, he would look at the concept art in video game guide books and art books for hours and dream of leaving his mark in projects and stories like they have through their art.

He's taught himself to use programs such as Procreate, Clip Studio Paint, and Nomad Sculpt for his work.

Commissioned work is his personal favorite. He likes having a clear objective for his art piece before starting it. But he also enjoys the variety of ways his art might be used for, whether it's website ads or posters or even covers for books or albums, he likes knowing his art is being seen. He also finds commercial work a great way to expand and stretch his creativity in ways he wouldn't think of by himself.

You could pay him to design your characters and do your concept art too.

What more can be said about this artist? He would urge you to look at his work and make your own opinion of him.
